# Privacy Policy: Central Park Tracker

_Last updated: 2026-10-26_

Central Park Tracker ("the app") helps you track which paths of Central Park you have
walked. This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

## The short version

- The app uses your location **only while a walk is active**, to detect which
  park paths you pass.
- Your progress is stored **only on your device**. It is never sent to us or to
  anyone else.
- There are **no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking**.

## Location data

- **What is collected:** your device's GPS location, while a walk session is
  running.
- **Why:** to match your position to Central Park's paths and mark them as
  walked, and to recognize when you have left the park so the session can stop.
- **When:** only during an active walk that you start. The app does not access
  your location when you are not on a walk. Tracking continues while the app is
  in the background so your phone can stay in your pocket during a walk; iOS
  shows a blue status indicator whenever this is happening.
- **Where it goes:** nowhere. Location is processed on your device to update your
  coverage. The app does not transmit your location or your routes off the
  device, and it does not keep a minute-by-minute trail. It stores only which
  path segments you have covered and the date each was first covered.

## Storage and your control

- Your coverage and walk history are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData
  (on-device database). This data is included in your normal encrypted iPhone
  backups (iCloud or Finder).
- You can **export** a backup file, **import** one, or **reset** all progress at
  any time from the in-app menu.
- You can revoke location access at any time in **Settings > Central Park Tracker >
  Location**. The app still opens and shows your existing progress, but cannot
  record new walks until access is restored.
- Deleting the app removes all of its on-device data.

## Data sharing

The app shares no personal data with anyone. It contains no advertising SDKs,
no analytics SDKs, and no third-party login.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information beyond
on-device location used for the walk feature described above.

## Map data

Path and park boundary data are © OpenStreetMap contributors, used under the
Open Database License (ODbL). The base map is provided by Apple Maps.
